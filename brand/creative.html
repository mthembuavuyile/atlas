03 — Export Standards

Asset Export Specs & QR Code Rules

Digital Export Specs

  • • Format: PNG-24 (Graphics) or WebP (Photos)
  • • Color Space: sRGB
  • • Resolution: 72 DPI (Screen)
  • • Scale: 2x export for retina screens

Branded QR Code Specs

  • • Module Color: Vylex Navy (#051B38)
  • • Foreground Accent: Vylex Orange (#FBA919)
  • • Quiet Zone: Minimum 4 modules margin
  • • Error Correction: Level M (15%) or H (30%)
